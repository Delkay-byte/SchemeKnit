    - heading "How the AI lesson plan generator works" [level=2]

        - heading "Refine and export" [level=3]

    - text: After The Draft
    - heading "The generated plan is a starting point, not the deliverable" [level=2]
    - text: A first draft is only useful if you can change it. Every plan lands in the workspace, where you edit it, check it, and take it out to the tools your school runs on.
    - heading "Edit the draft" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Every generated plan opens in the document editor. Rewrite sections, cut activities, and change wording before anyone else sees it.
    - heading "Evaluate the text" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Run a generated plan or its student-facing materials through an evaluator and read its feedback on the document.
    - heading "Apply the revisions" [level=3]
    - text: Requires Pro
    - paragraph: Turn an evaluator's suggestions into edits to the document in one step, instead of making each change by hand.
    - heading "Export where you teach" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Send the finished plan to Google Docs or Microsoft Word.
    - heading "Differentiate for your class" [level=3]
    - text: Requires Pro
    - paragraph: Adapt a plan's reading level and supports for the students in front of you, either in place or as a new version.
    - heading "Revise with Erasmus" [level=3]
    - text: Requires Pro
    - paragraph: Talk to the document. Ask for a shorter warm-up, a different hook, or an extra formative check, and apply the result.
