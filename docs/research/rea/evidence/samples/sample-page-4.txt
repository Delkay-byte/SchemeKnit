Activity 1 - Direct Instruction with Annotated Diagram (15 min):
• Using the blank outline already on the whiteboard, add labels one by one as you
explain each structure, writing the name, drawing an arrow to the part, and stating its
function aloud.
Key Structures and Functions (from Teacher Manual):
• Ovaries: A pair of small, almond-shaped organs. They produce eggs (ova) and
female sex hormones (oestrogen and progesterone).
• Oviducts (Fallopian Tubes): Two narrow tubes connecting the ovaries to the
uterus. They are the site of fertilisation and carry the fertilised egg (zygote) to the
uterus.
• Uterus (Womb): A hollow, muscular organ lined with the endometrium. It is the
site of implantation; it supplies nourishment and oxygen to the foetus and provides
protection and support throughout pregnancy.
• Cervix: The lower, narrow part of the uterus that connects to the vagina. It dilates
during childbirth to allow the baby to pass through.
• Vagina: The muscular canal connecting the cervix to the outside of the body. It is
the passage for sperm during copulation, for menstrual blood, and for the baby
during birth.
• Vulva: The external female genitalia that encloses and protects the openings of
the vagina and urethra.
