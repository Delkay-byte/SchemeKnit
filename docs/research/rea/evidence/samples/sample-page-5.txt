Activity 2 - Think-Pair-Share: Matching Structures to Functions (10 min):
• Each learner individually reads the six structures and their functions from their
notebook for 2 minutes.
• Learners then pair with the person next to them (pairs should be mixed-gender
where seating allows) and take turns quizzing each other:
Partner A names a structure; Partner B states its function — then swap.
• After pairing, selected pairs (teacher calls on both a male and a female learner
from different pairs) share one structure-function link with the class, which the
teacher briefly confirms or corrects on the whiteboard.
• Close the activity with this question for the whole class to answer in their books in
one sentence:
'Which part of the female reproductive system do you think has the most complex role,
and why?'
Learners who finish quickly should add a second sentence explaining what would
happen to reproduction if that part were absent.
Activity 3 - Group Investigation: Reproductive Processes from Copulation to Birth
(20 min):
Organise the class into 5 mixed-ability, mixed-gender groups of 6 learners each.
Assign each group a process sequence card (written on the whiteboard):
Group 1: Copulation and Fertilisation
Group 2: Implantation and Early Development
Group 3: First and Second Trimester Foetal Development
Group 4: Third Trimester, the Placenta, and Birth
Group 5: Breastfeeding and the Role of the Female Reproductive System After Birth
Each group discusses their assigned process for 8 minutes, using the whiteboard
diagram and their notebook notes as reference, then prepares a 2-minute verbal
presentation.
