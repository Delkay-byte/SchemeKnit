Key Assessment
Formative Assessment:
Assessment Mode: Oral questioning, paired quizzing, group presentation, and
individual written Exit Ticket
DOK Level 1 - Recall Tasks:
- Name the six main structures of the female reproductive system.
- State the function of the ovaries.
- Identify where fertilisation takes place in the female reproductive system.
DOK Level 2 - Application Tasks:
- Draw and label at least five parts of the female reproductive system, then write one
function beside each labelled part.
- Describe the journey of a fertilised egg from fertilisation to implantation, naming each
structure it passes through and explaining what occurs at each stage.
DOK Level 3 - Reasoning Task:
- Evaluate the following statement and justify your answer with evidence from the lesson:
'The uterus is the most important organ in the female reproductive system.' Do you agree
or disagree? Use specific structural and functional evidence to support your position.
Summative Assessment:
Task:
- Learners complete the following written task (aligned to Teacher Manual Week 11
Sample Assessment): 'Describe the structure of the female reproductive system and give at
least one function of at least five parts. Then describe the process of reproduction in
humans from copulation to birth.'
Rubric:
Excellent (4): Accurately labels and describes at least six structures with correct, detailed
functions; provides a clear, sequenced account of all reproductive processes from
copulation to birth including implantation, trimester development, placenta role, and
breastfeeding; uses precise scientific vocabulary throughout.
Good (3): Labels and describes five structures with mostly correct functions; describes
most reproductive processes in the correct sequence with minor omissions; uses scientific
vocabulary appropriately.
Satisfactory (2): Labels and describes three to four structures with partially correct
functions; describes some reproductive processes but with gaps or sequencing errors;
