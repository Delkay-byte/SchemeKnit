CLOSURE
Summary (5 min):
• Recap the key points covered in today's lesson:
'Today we identified and described the six main structures of the female reproductive
system: the ovaries, oviducts, uterus, cervix, vagina, and vulva — and we explained the
specific function each part performs.'
'We also traced the sequence of reproductive processes: copulation, fertilisation in the
oviduct, implantation in the uterus, foetal development across three trimesters, the role
of the placenta in nourishing the foetus, birth, and breastfeeding.'
• Emphasise two key facts to commit to memory:
'The oviduct is where fertilisation occurs — not the uterus.'
'The uterus is the site of implantation, development, nourishment, and protection of the
foetus.'
• Remind learners: 'Before the next lesson, please revise today's notes on the
female reproductive system — especially the six structures and their functions — as
we will build on this knowledge when we study the male reproductive system and
compare the two systems.'
