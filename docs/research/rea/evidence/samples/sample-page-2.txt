Key Notes on Differentiation
Identify the different learner needs in your class and make notes on how to cater for them
during the lesson
Learner Needs Identified:
1. High-Achieving Learners (approximately 8 learners):
• Extend beyond labelling to write detailed functional descriptions and link structures to
processes such as implantation and foetal nourishment.
• Take on peer-teaching roles during group presentations, explaining structures and
processes to classmates.
• Respond to higher-order questions about what would happen if a specific organ were
absent or damaged.
2. Average Learners (approximately 16 learners):
• Complete the core task of labelling at least five parts of the female reproductive system
and stating one function for each.
• Participate actively in guided group work and contribute to group presentations.
• Use the whiteboard diagram and teacher-provided vocabulary list as reference during
tasks.
3. Struggling Learners (approximately 5 learners):
• Receive a partially completed diagram with some labels already filled in; task is to
complete remaining labels using a word bank.
• Focus on identifying and stating the function of three key structures: ovaries, uterus, and
vagina.
• Receive additional teacher attention and simplified explanations using real-life analogies
(e.g. the uterus as a protective room for the baby).
