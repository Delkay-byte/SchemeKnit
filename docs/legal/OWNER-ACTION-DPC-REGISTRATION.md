## 1. Data Protection Commission registration

The owner has not supplied a confirmed Data Protection Commission (DPC)
registration status, number or certificate.

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843):

- **Section 27** makes registration of a data controller a substantive
  compliance requirement for processing personal data.
- **Section 53** deals with the related registration/authorisation framework
  and associated obligations.

Removing or omitting a public certificate page does not remove this legal
obligation. The public Privacy Policy correctly makes no claim that BloomCore
Technologies is DPC-registered.

Owner action:

1. Confirm whether BloomCore Technologies is registered with the Ghana Data
   Protection Commission.
2. If registered, supply the verified registration number and certificate for
   controlled publication.
3. If not registered, obtain Ghanaian legal/compliance advice and complete the
   required registration before relying on the current processing.
4. Do not describe the status as “pending” unless an application has actually
   been submitted and the owner confirms it.

The Data Retention Policy distinguishes:

- Act 843 section 24 necessity;
- Act 915 section 27 tax-record retention (at least six years from the relevant
  date, longer in specified unresolved matters);
- Act 772 section 8 six-year electronic-record rule where another law requires
  retention.

The following currently have no scheduled expiry and require separately
justified, approved and tested retention/deletion decisions:

- stored export files;
- Paystack webhook events;
- security/login/audit records;
