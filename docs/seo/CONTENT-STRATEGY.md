# SEO Content Strategy

Date: 2026-10-31  
Status: research plan, not verified search-volume data

## Audience

Ghanaian KG, primary and JHS teachers; school administrators; teachers
preparing NaCCA/GES-aligned lesson plans from a Scheme of Learning.

## Search-intent topics

Treat these as research hypotheses, not confirmed keyword volumes:

- lesson plan generator Ghana
- Ghana basic school lesson planning
- Scheme of Learning Ghana
- NaCCA curriculum lesson planning
- KG lesson planning Ghana
- primary school lesson planning Ghana
- JHS lesson planning Ghana
- teacher lesson preparation tools
- Ghana teacher lesson plan template
- GES lesson plan format
- Scheme of Learning to lesson plans
- Basic 9 lesson plans Ghana

## Recommended public pages

Build genuinely useful pages, not keyword variants:

1. **Ghana lesson plan generator** — how TeachFlow turns a Scheme of Learning
   into indicator-level plans.
2. **Scheme of Learning guide** — what a Ghanaian scheme contains, how strands,
   sub-strands, content standards and indicators relate.
3. **KG lesson planning** — age-appropriate activity-based planning.
4. **Primary lesson planning** — subject-specific examples.
5. **JHS lesson planning** — including Basic 9 core subjects.
6. **GES/NaCCA lesson-plan format** — explain the fields and why they exist.
7. **Teacher preparation workflow** — upload, review, configure, generate,
   edit, export.
8. **Pricing and credits** — factual package/credit explanation.

Each page needs original, useful content, a clear audience, examples from real
curriculum structures, and internal links to the product. Do not create thin
pages that only repeat keywords.

## E-E-A-T requirements

- Show real product behavior, not invented claims.
- Use accurate Ghanaian curriculum terminology.
- Do not claim official NaCCA/GES endorsement.
- Publish contact and legal pages.
- Date substantive content updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, ratings or user counts.

## Measurement

Only after staging/domain/Search Console access:

- index coverage,
- queries and impressions,
- click-through rate,
- Core Web Vitals,
- conversion from public page to signup.

No ranking or traffic result is claimed now.
